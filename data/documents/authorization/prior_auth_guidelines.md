# HealthSure Prior Authorization Guidelines 2026

## MRI prior authorization
MRI scans require prior authorization when performed as an outpatient advanced imaging procedure.
Approval requires documentation of medical necessity, the physician order, symptoms, prior
conservative treatment and relevant clinical notes. The ordering provider usually submits the request.

## Decisions and timing
Standard requests are decided within 15 calendar days. If prior authorization is approved before the
service, the claim is processed under plan benefits. If it is missing, the claim may be denied with
reason code PA_MISSING.

## Retroactive review
When a claim is denied for PA_MISSING, the ordering provider may request retroactive review as part
of an appeal by submitting the same medical-necessity documentation.

## Urgent review
Urgent requests are escalated to clinical review and decided within 72 hours when documentation is
incomplete or symptoms suggest immediate medical risk.
