# HealthSure Gold 2026 Member Policy

## Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET)
MRI scans are covered when medically necessary and ordered by an in-network provider.
Outpatient advanced imaging requires prior authorization before the service date.
Advanced imaging is subject to the annual deductible and coinsurance. After the deductible is met,
the plan pays 80% and the member pays 20% coinsurance.

## Deductible
The annual individual deductible is $500. The member pays eligible expenses until the deductible is met.

## Coinsurance
After the deductible is met, covered services are paid at 80% by the plan and 20% by the member.

## Out-of-pocket maximum
The annual out-of-pocket maximum is $5,000 for in-network covered services. After it is reached,
the plan pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the plan year.

## Physical therapy
Outpatient physical therapy is covered up to 30 visits per plan year when ordered by a physician.
Prior authorization is not required.

## Exclusions
Experimental procedures and cosmetic procedures are not covered. Services that required prior
authorization but were performed without it may be denied.

## Appeals
Members may appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the denial notice. Include the denial notice,
the ordering physician's notes and any supporting medical records.
